The real gap

This is for you if
  • Marketing matters to your growth, and it’s outgrown the founder
  • You have people doing the work, but nobody leading it

It’s not for you if you need someone to run your Instagram, tick off tasks or nod along.

How I think

Most marketing leaders think like marketers. I think like an owner.

Before I ask what you should post, I ask how the business makes money, where the growth is and what’s actually in the way. So I don’t care how busy your marketing looks. I care what it does for the business.

I look at the whole business, not just the marketing.

Offer, pricing, sales process, positioning. Often the fix isn’t another campaign. It’s hiding in one of those.

I trust your customers over anyone’s opinion. Including mine.

For BSharp, that meant 130+ sales calls, interviews, surveys and reviews before I made a single recommendation.

I’m on your side of the table.

I don’t sell ads, SEO or a team. So I can tell you when an agency is brilliant, when it’s coasting, and when the problem isn’t marketing at all.

I’m picky about who touches your business.

My specialist circle is small on purpose: two top-tier SEO and paid media agencies, a UX designer and analytics experts. If I wouldn’t hire them myself, they won’t touch yours.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I have to commit to a retainer?

No. The audit stands on its own. Take the roadmap and run with it, or have me lead it. Your call.

How is this different from an agency?

An agency sells you services. I decide which services you actually need, then hold whoever delivers them to account. I answer to you, not to a media budget.

Will you work with our existing team and vendors?

Yes. If they’re good, I’ll make them better with clearer briefs and faster decisions. If they’re not, I’ll tell you, and help you replace them.

Do you do the work yourself?

I personally lead strategy, positioning, messaging and creative direction. Execution goes to your team, your vendors or my specialists.

Is copywriting included?

Messaging direction and copy review, yes. Big projects like a full website rewrite are scoped separately so they get proper attention.

Who do you work with?

Scaling B2B and service businesses with a strong offer, real expertise and marketing that hasn’t caught up yet.
